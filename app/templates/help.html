The dashboard numbers

Four cards sit at the top, all about this month unless they say otherwise:

  • Sales today — money taken today, with the number of transactions.
  • Sales this month — everything sold since the first of the month.
  • Gross profit (month) — sales minus the cost of the goods sold. The line under it shows that cost of goods (COGS).
  • Net after expenses — gross profit minus the expenses you logged.

Below the cards you get the daily profit trend, your best sellers, recent sales and the items that are running low. Tax is never counted as profit: it is collected, not earned.

Products and inventory

Inventory is the list of everything you sell. Each product carries a name, an optional SKU, a category, a supplier, a cost price, a selling price and a reorder level.

  • Cost price is what the item costs you. It is the number that makes profit truthful, so keep it current.
  • Selling price is what you charge. The product page shows the margin between the two.
  • Reorder level is the point where the dashboard starts calling the item low on stock.
  • Archive hides a product from the sale screen without deleting its history.

Receiving stock

Every delivery goes under Stock: choose the product, the quantity that arrived and the unit cost you paid. Stock on hand goes up immediately.

The latest purchase price becomes the product's cost price, so the profit reported on the next sale uses the price you actually paid most recently. Change the cost price by hand on the product if you ever need to.

Recording a sale

Sales → New sale. Add one line per item with its quantity and unit price, then save. A few things happen automatically:

  • Stock on hand drops by the quantity sold.
  • The cost of goods is taken from each product's cost price, so gross profit is accurate.
  • A discount comes off the subtotal before tax. Tax, when it is switched on in Settings, is added on top of the discounted amount.

The sale list shows the total, the cost of goods and the profit per sale, so you can see which transactions actually made money.

Expenses

Rent, transport, data, salaries, anything you spend to keep the shop running. Log the date, what it was for and the amount. Expenses do not touch stock or sales: they come off gross profit to give the net figure on the dashboard.

Accounts, roles and sign-ups

  • Administrator — everything a member can do, plus Settings (store name, currency, tax) and Admin (accounts, roles, sign-ups, store code).
  • Member — records sales, takes in stock, manages products and logs expenses.
  • Sign-ups — an administrator can allow or close new accounts, share the invite link, and create a new store code.
  • Store code — optional at sign-up. Someone with the invite link gets it filled in for them.
  • Everyone shares one set of books: all accounts see the same sales, stock and expenses.
